You are one of 3 bidders in a single-item sealed-bid auction. Your private value is drawn from {'private_range': [0, 49], 'common_range': None, 'signal_noise': None}, and you must submit exactly one numeric bid from the menu 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 49. The highest bid wins the item, and the winner pays the second-highest bid; if there is a tie for the highest bid, the winner is chosen at random. Your payoff is your value minus your payment if you win, and 0 if you do not win. Worked example: if you bid 20, another bidder bids 15, and the third bids 10, then you win and pay 15. Because there is no feedback and only one auction round, choose the bid that best matches your private value.
