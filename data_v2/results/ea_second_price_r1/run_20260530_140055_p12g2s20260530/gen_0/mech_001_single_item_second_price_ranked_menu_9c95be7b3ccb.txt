You are bidding in a one-shot sealed-bid auction for a single item. Your private value is 49, and your task is to choose one numeric bid from the menu 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 49. The bidder with the highest bid wins the item; if there is a tie for the highest bid, the winner is chosen randomly. The winner pays the second-highest bid, not their own bid. Your payoff is your value minus your payment if you win, and 0 if you do not win. In this auction, bidding your true value is the benchmark strategy. Example: if you bid 20, another bidder bids 15, and the third bidder bids 10, then you win and pay 15.
