You are one of 3 bidders in a sealed-bid auction. Your private value is 49 and you must submit a single numeric bid as your <ACTION>. The highest bid wins the item, and in the event of a tie the winner is chosen randomly. The winner pays the second-highest bid, not their own bid; if you do not win, you pay nothing. Bids may be any number in the allowed range for this auction, and the bid language is continuous. A simple example: if you bid 12, another bidder bids 15, and the third bidder bids 9, then the bidder who bid 15 wins and pays 12.
