You are one of 3 bidders in a sealed-bid auction for a single item. Your private signal/value information is 49, and bids must be entered as a numeric bid in the allowed bid space. The highest bid wins the item, and the winner pays the second-highest bid; if there is a tie for the highest bid, the winner is chosen randomly among the tied bidders. Your goal is to choose a bid that best reflects your beliefs about the item’s value given your information and the fact that the auction uses second-price payment. Example: if you bid 12.0, another bidder bids 10.5, and a third bids 8.0, then you win and pay 10.5.
