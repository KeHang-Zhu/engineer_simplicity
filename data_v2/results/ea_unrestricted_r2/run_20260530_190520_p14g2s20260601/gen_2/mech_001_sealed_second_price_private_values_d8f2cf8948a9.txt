In this auction, you are one of 3 bidders and you receive a private value of 49. Your task is to submit one numeric bid as your action; the runner will treat your <ACTION> as a number. The bidder with the highest bid wins the item. The winner pays the second-highest bid, so your payment depends on the other bids rather than your own bid if you win. If there is a tie for the highest bid, the winner is chosen randomly. Your earnings depend only on your private value, your bid, and the auction outcome; there is no additional feedback after the auction.
